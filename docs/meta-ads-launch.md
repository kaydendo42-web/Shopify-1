## The 5 ads (2 video + 3 static)

### Tone direction across all 5
- **No medical claims** — TGA / ACCC compliant
- **No celebrity faces** — substitute with anon-style framing (no Kim K likeness)
- **Perimenopause-coded** — soft light, linen, books, candle, ceramic mug. NEVER bright pop-music UGC.
- **Voiceover tone:** Australian female, 40s, warm-low register. Think Cate Blanchett doing skincare.
