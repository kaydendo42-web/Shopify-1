## PHASE 1: PRODUCT RESEARCH

### Hard Criteria (ALL must be met)
- [ ] At least **1 competitor doing $300k+/month** selling something similar
- [ ] **$30+ gross margin** (selling price minus COGS including shipping)
- [ ] **3x COGS minimum** (e.g. costs $40 all-in → sell for $120+)
- [ ] **30,000+ monthly searches** on main Google keyword
- [ ] Strong **gift potential** — emotional, impulse-buy friendly, broad demographic

### Purple Ocean Positioning (required)
- Do NOT sell to everyone → sell to a **hyper-specific demographic** within a proven market
- Formula: `[Proven product category]` + `[Specific audience/use case/identity]`
- Examples:
  - Carnivore supplements → for postpartum women
  - Nootropics → for entrepreneurs
  - Massage gun → for nurses on night shifts
- **Your angle must be unique enough that a customer sees it and thinks "that's for ME"**

### Validation Methods (use at least 2)
| Method | Tool | Green Light Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Competitor traffic | SimilarWeb Chrome extension | 300k+ visits/month, trending up |
| Ad volume | GetHookd / Meta Ad Library | 100+ active ads from one competitor |
| Search demand | Google Ads Keyword Planner (free) | 30k+ monthly searches on main term |
| Amazon volume | Helium 10 | Strong BSR + review velocity |

### Margin Calculator
```

Shipping Cost:         $___

Gross Margin:          $___   ← must be $30+

```

### Gift Product Checklist
- [ ] Could someone buy this for a friend/family member?
- [ ] Does it have a "wow" factor when seen?
- [ ] Is the price point in a comfortable gifting range ($40-$120)?
- [ ] Does it solve a relatable problem people would share?
- [ ] Would someone tag a friend in an ad for this?

## PHASE 2: FOUNDATIONAL DOCS (DO THIS BEFORE ANY COPY)

> Run these 4 research docs using Claude (Sonnet or Opus) BEFORE writing a single word of copy or building the store. Feed outputs as PDFs into all future copy work.

### Doc 1 — Customer Avatar
Prompt Claude with your product + purple ocean angle and ask it to build:
- Demographics (age, gender, income, location)
- Psychographics (values, identity, aspirations)
- Day in the life
- Where they hang out online
- What they've already tried and why it failed

### Doc 2 — Pain Points & Emotional Triggers
- Top 5 deep pains (not surface-level)
- Emotional language they use (pull from Reddit, Amazon reviews, Facebook groups)
- What they're embarrassed about
- What they're afraid of
- What they desperately want

### Doc 3 — Objections & Beliefs
- Why they haven't bought something like this before
- Price objections
- Trust/skepticism objections
- "Will this actually work for me?" objections
- How to pre-handle each one in copy

### Doc 4 — Messaging Angles
- 5-10 ad angles derived from the research above
- Each angle = a different emotional hook or entry point
- Examples: fear angle, aspiration angle, social proof angle, "us vs them" angle, curiosity angle

> **Save all 4 as PDFs. Reference them for EVERYTHING downstream.**

## PHASE 4: AD CREATIVE PRODUCTION

### Creative Mix
- **80% Swipes** — find winning competitor ads on GetHookd or Meta Ad Library, remake for your product
- **20% Originals** — use emotional angles from foundational docs that competitors haven't touched

### Start With Images First
- Cheaper and faster to produce
- Lower CPMs = more data per dollar
- Test more messaging concepts faster
- Once a winning message is found → convert to video

## PHASE 6: DATA READING & DECISION MAKING

### Core Rule: Measure in SPEND, not days
- Make no real decisions until **$100-150 USD spent** minimum
- Full read at **$200-300 USD total spend**
- At $63 USD/day that's roughly **3-5 days per test**

### Key Metrics to Watch
| Metric | What It Tells You |
|---|---|
| CPM | How competitive your audience/creative is |
| CTR (link) | Is the ad stopping the scroll + getting clicks? |
| CPC | Cost per click — should be $1-3 ideally |
| Add to Cart rate | Is the page compelling? |
| Checkout initiated | Are people getting stuck? |
| ROAS | Revenue ÷ ad spend — needs to be above breakeven |
| CPA | Cost per acquisition — compare to gross margin |

### Breakeven ROAS Formula
```
Breakeven ROAS = Selling Price ÷ Gross Margin
Example: Sell at $100, margin $60 → Breakeven ROAS = 100/60 = 1.67x
```

### Cut Rules
| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Ad spent past breakeven CPA with zero sales | Kill immediately |
| Ad below breakeven ROAS and trending down | Kill |
| Haven't spent $100 USD yet | Do NOT touch, wait |
| Product spent $200 USD, no sales, no signals | Kill product, move on |

### Scale Rules
| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Ad set profitable and consistent | Increase budget 20-30% every 2-3 days |
| Winning concept identified | Create 3-5 more variations of that concept |
| Winning image | Convert to video version |
| Winning message | Use it as your landing page headline |
