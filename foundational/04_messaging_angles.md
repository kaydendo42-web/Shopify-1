# Foundational Doc 4 — Messaging Angles

> 10 ad concepts, each a distinct emotional entry point. Each angle gets 3–6 creative variations at launch. The winner becomes the page headline.

---

## Angle 1 — The Mirror Moment (Identity)
**Emotional lever:** Recognition / loss of self
**Hook:** "I looked tired in every photo — even when I wasn't."
**Headline:** *You don't want to look younger. You want to look like yourself, on your best day.*
**Visual:** Close-up of 45-year-old woman applying mask, eyes closed, no makeup. Soft window light. Robe.
**CTA:** "Find your face again"
**Best for:** Cold traffic top-of-funnel. Highest stop rate.

## Angle 2 — The Skincare Graveyard (Skeptic)
**Emotional lever:** Frustration with past failures
**Hook:** "I counted what's in my bathroom drawer. $1,400 of products that don't work."
**Headline:** *Stop layering serums hoping one finally works. Treat the layer underneath.*
**Visual:** Overhead flatlay of 12 half-used serums beside a single mask. Subtitle: "What if it was just one device?"
**CTA:** "Try the only one I kept"
**Best for:** Mid-funnel retargeting of beauty-interest audiences.

## Angle 3 — The Phase (Acknowledge perimenopause directly)
**Emotional lever:** Permission / being seen
**Hook:** "Your skincare didn't stop working. Your skin moved into a new phase."
**Headline:** *Designed for the decade your skin changes.*
**Visual:** Woman 47ish, kitchen window light, towel-dried hair, looking at her phone calmly. Voiceover or text overlay.
**CTA:** "Built for this phase"
**Best for:** Warm audiences. Brand-builder. Press.

## Angle 4 — The Quiet Ritual (Lifestyle / wellness)
**Emotional lever:** Permission to rest
**Hook:** "10 minutes of nobody-needs-anything time."
**Headline:** *The only ritual where your face is the only thing you have to think about.*
**Visual:** Woman in bath or on chaise, mask on, eyes closed, glass of water beside. Candle. Book. No phone.
**CTA:** "Take your 10 minutes"
**Best for:** Sunday-evening Insta audiences. Slow-scroll. Aspirational.

## Angle 5 — The Spa Maths (Price logic)
**Emotional lever:** Smart purchasing
**Hook:** "8 LED facials at my dermatologist = $1,600. One device at home = lifetime."
**Headline:** *The same technology. None of the appointments.*
**Visual:** Split screen — clinic chair / home bedroom. Comparison numbers floating.
**CTA:** "Skip the clinic"
**Best for:** Late-stage funnel. Comparison shoppers. Justifies the spend.

## Angle 6 — The Partner Notice (Social proof)
**Emotional lever:** Quiet validation
**Hook:** "My husband asked if I'd had something done. I hadn't."
**Headline:** *People notice. They just can't tell why.*
**Visual:** UGC-style selfie video. Real customer, real lighting, casual reveal of mask in drawer.
**CTA:** "Get the look they can't pinpoint"
**Best for:** Testimonial-led creative. Powerful in Stories format.

## Angle 7 — The Anti-Anti-Ageing (Ideology / belief reframe)
**Emotional lever:** Permission to care without guilt
**Hook:** "I'm not trying to look 25. I'm trying to look 47 — rested."
**Headline:** *Not anti-ageing. Pro-you.*
**Visual:** Confident 50-year-old face direct to camera, slight smile, no retouching obvious.
**CTA:** "Ritual, not refusal"
**Best for:** Brand campaigns. PR. Builds long-term equity.

## Angle 8 — The Founder Story (Trust / authenticity)
**Emotional lever:** Believability
**Hook:** "I'm 47. I built this because nothing in the chemist worked for me anymore."
**Headline:** *Built by a woman in the same phase as you.*
**Visual:** Founder face piece-to-camera, real desk, real product, real story.
**CTA:** "Hear why I made this"
**Best for:** Press. About page. Welcome email.

## Angle 9 — The Science Drop (Authority for the skeptic)
**Emotional lever:** Validate her research-mode
**Hook:** "635nm + 830nm. These are the wavelengths your skin actually responds to."
**Headline:** *Clinical-grade light therapy. At home.*
**Visual:** Animated wavelength diagram on a clean background. Or microscope-style collagen footage.
**CTA:** "See the science"
**Best for:** Mid-funnel for analytical audiences. Older demo on FB.

## Angle 10 — The Gift Angle (Mother's Day, Birthday, Q4)
**Emotional lever:** Permission via others
**Hook:** "She'd never buy it for herself."
**Headline:** *The Mother's Day gift she's saved twice and never tapped.*
**Visual:** Partner / daughter hands gift box to woman. Bow. Gift card visible: "for your 10 minutes."
**CTA:** "Gift the ritual"
**Best for:** Q4. Mother's Day campaigns. April–May AU surge.

## Tone calibration cheat sheet

| Want to say | Don't say | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "anti-ageing" | ❌ | "support midlife skin" |
| "wrinkle removal" | ❌ | "firmer-looking skin" |
| "miracle" | ❌ | "noticeable in 28 days" |
| "for older women" | ❌ | "for women in the phase" / "for women 40+" |
| "fight signs of ageing" | ❌ | "show up for your skin" |
| "look 10 years younger" | ❌ | "look like yourself, rested" |

## Sign-off — the brand thesis (north star)

> **We're not selling a mask. We're selling a ten-minute window where she's not needed by anyone. The mask is what makes the window possible — and the visible change in her skin is what proves she didn't imagine the time was earned.**

Every headline, image, FAQ, and email returns to this thesis.
